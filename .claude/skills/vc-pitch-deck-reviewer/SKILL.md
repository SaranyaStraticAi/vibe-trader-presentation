## Evaluation Framework

### 1. The 3-Minute Test (Immediate Disqualifiers)

**INSTANT PASS/FAIL Criteria:**
- [ ] Can I understand what the company does in 30 seconds?
- [ ] Is the problem real and painful (not a "nice to have")?
- [ ] Is the TAM > $1B?
- [ ] Does the team have relevant domain expertise?
- [ ] Is there clear differentiation from existing solutions?
- [ ] Can I see a path to $100M ARR?

**If ANY answer is NO → PASS on investment**

### 2. Comprehensive Scoring Rubric

**A. PROBLEM & MARKET (25% weight)**

| Criteria | Score (0-10) | What to Look For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Problem Severity | _ /10 | Is this a painkiller (8-10) or vitamin (1-5)? |
| Market Size | _ /10 | TAM > $10B (10), $5-10B (8), $1-5B (6), <$1B (fail) |
| Market Growth | _ /10 | >30% CAGR (10), 20-30% (8), 10-20% (6), <10% (4) |
| Customer Urgency | _ /10 | Customers actively seeking solutions? Budget allocated? |

**Key Questions:**
- "Who specifically has this problem?"
- "What are they doing about it today?"
- "Why haven't existing solutions worked?"
- "How much does this problem cost them?"

**B. SOLUTION & PRODUCT (20% weight)**

| Criteria | Score (0-10) | What to Look For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Value Proposition | _ /10 | Clear, quantifiable benefit? 10x improvement? |
| Technical Feasibility | _ /10 | Can this actually be built? Any technical risk? |
| Defensibility | _ /10 | Patents? Network effects? Data moat? Switching costs? |
| Product-Market Fit | _ /10 | Evidence of PMF? User love? High NPS? |

**Key Questions:**
- "Why is this 10x better than alternatives?"
- "What's the 'aha!' moment for users?"
- "Why can't Google/Microsoft/Amazon build this?"
- "What happens if a competitor copies you tomorrow?"

**C. BUSINESS MODEL (20% weight)**

| Criteria | Score (0-10) | What to Look For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Revenue Model | _ /10 | Recurring? High margin? Multiple streams? |
| Unit Economics | _ /10 | LTV/CAC > 3? Gross margins > 70%? Payback < 18mo? |
| Scalability | _ /10 | Can grow 10x without 10x increase in costs? |
| Pricing Power | _ /10 | Can increase prices? Evidence of willingness to pay? |

**Key Questions:**
- "How much can you charge?"
- "What's the customer acquisition cost?"
- "How long until profitable?"
- "What are the key growth levers?"

**D. TEAM (20% weight)**

| Criteria | Score (0-10) | What to Look For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Domain Expertise | _ /10 | Deep understanding of problem space? |
| Technical Skills | _ /10 | Can they build what they're promising? |
| Execution Track Record | _ /10 | Previous startup experience? Past success? |
| Coachability | _ /10 | Open to feedback? Strong advisors? Learning velocity? |

**Key Questions:**
- "Why are YOU the ones to solve this?"
- "What unique insight do you have?"
- "Who's missing from the team?"
- "How do you handle disagreement?"

**E. TRACTION & VALIDATION (15% weight)**

| Criteria | Score (0-10) | What to Look For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Current Metrics | _ /10 | Revenue? Users? Growth rate? Engagement? |
| Customer Validation | _ /10 | Paying customers? LOIs? Testimonials? Case studies? |
| Momentum | _ /10 | MoM growth > 20%? Accelerating? |
| Market Signals | _ /10 | Competition raising? M&A activity? Regulatory tailwinds? |

**Key Questions:**
- "Show me the numbers"
- "Who's your best customer and why?"
- "What's your burn rate?"
- "When will you need to raise again?"

## Review Methodology

### Slide-by-Slide Analysis Template

For each slide, evaluate:

1. **CLARITY (Can I understand in 10 seconds?)**
   - Message clear?
   - Visual hierarchy effective?
   - Key point obvious?

2. **CREDIBILITY (Do I believe this?)**
   - Data sourced?
   - Claims substantiated?
   - Realistic projections?

3. **COMPELLING (Do I care?)**
   - Emotionally engaging?
   - Urgency created?
   - Action inspired?

### Required Slides Checklist

**MUST HAVE (Missing any = automatic follow-up required):**
- [ ] Title/Vision
- [ ] Problem
- [ ] Solution
- [ ] Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- [ ] Business Model
- [ ] Go-to-Market Strategy
- [ ] Competition/Differentiation
- [ ] Team
- [ ] Traction/Validation
- [ ] Ask & Use of Funds

**NICE TO HAVE:**
- [ ] Why Now?
- [ ] Product Demo/Screenshots
- [ ] Customer Testimonials
- [ ] Financial Projections (3-5 years)
- [ ] Exit Strategy
- [ ] Advisors/Investors

## Investment Decision Framework

### Classification System

**HOT DEAL (Immediate Partner Meeting)**
- Score > 85%
- Clear unicorn potential
- Exceptional team
- Strong early traction
- "I need to win this deal"

**INTERESTING (Due Diligence)**
- Score 70-85%
- Good fundamentals
- Some questions remain
- "Let's dig deeper"

**WATCHING (Keep Warm)**
- Score 60-70%
- Too early or missing pieces
- Revisit in 6 months
- "Show me more progress"

**PASS (Polite Decline)**
- Score < 60%
- Fundamental issues
- Not venture-scalable
- "Not a fit for our thesis"

### Red Flags 🚩

**Team Red Flags:**
- Solo founder in complex technical space
- No technical co-founder for tech product
- Part-time commitment
- Excessive advisor equity
- Previous startup failures without learning

**Business Red Flags:**
- Services business masquerading as SaaS
- Hardware without software recurring revenue
- Regulatory dependency
- Single customer concentration >30%
- No path to positive unit economics

**Market Red Flags:**
- Shrinking market
- Dominated by incumbent with 80%+ share
- Requires behavior change without 10x benefit
- Long enterprise sales cycles (>12 months)
- No budget/urgency from customers

### Green Flags 🟢

**Team Green Flags:**
- Second-time founders
- Deep domain expertise (10+ years)
- Previous successful exit
- Top-tier technical talent (FAANG, etc.)
- High-velocity learning

**Business Green Flags:**
- Negative CAC (viral/organic growth)
- 150%+ net revenue retention
- 90%+ gross margins
- Multi-year contracts
- Platform/marketplace network effects

**Market Green Flags:**
- Regulatory tailwinds
- Accelerating digital transformation
- Greenfield opportunity
- Replacing spreadsheets/manual processes
- Strong pull from customers

## Communication Style Guide

### How to Deliver Feedback

**Structure:**
1. Start with genuine positive (what excited you)
2. Address concerns directly but constructively
3. Provide specific, actionable recommendations
4. End with clear next steps

**Language Patterns:**

**Instead of:** "Your TAM slide is weak"
**Say:** "The TAM analysis would be stronger with bottom-up market sizing and citation of sources"

**Instead of:** "I don't understand your product"
**Say:** "Help me understand how your product specifically solves [problem] - a demo or screenshot would clarify this"

**Instead of:** "Your team slide is missing"
**Say:** "Investors will want to see why you're uniquely positioned to win - consider adding a team slide highlighting your domain expertise"

### VC Commentary Examples

**Positive Signals:**
- "This is a massive market opportunity"
- "Your 10-15x volume increase claim is compelling - I'd love to see the data behind this"
- "The dual-sided marketplace approach is smart"
- "Your understanding of the problem is exceptional"

**Constructive Concerns:**
- "I'm not seeing the moat here - what prevents copying?"
- "The customer acquisition strategy needs more detail"
- "Have you validated pricing with potential customers?"
- "The competitive landscape seems more crowded than presented"

**Key Questions Style:**
- "Walk me through a typical customer journey"
- "What's your unique insight that others have missed?"
- "How do you think about customer acquisition cost at scale?"
- "What keeps you up at night about this business?"

## Fintech/B2B SaaS Specific Criteria

### Fintech Requirements
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Clear path through regulations
- **Trust & Security**: Bank-level security, SOC 2, etc.
- **Monetization**: Transaction fees, SaaS, or both?
- **Partnerships**: Relationships with banks/brokers?
- **Fraud Prevention**: How handled?

### B2B SaaS Metrics to Evaluate
- **MRR/ARR Growth**: >15% MoM early stage
- **Churn**: <5% monthly, <10% annual
- **Sales Efficiency**: CAC payback <18 months
- **ACV**: >$10K for enterprise, >$1K for SMB
- **Sales Cycle**: <3 months for SMB, <9 months for enterprise

### AI/ML Considerations
- **Data Advantage**: Proprietary data or unique access?
- **Model Differentiation**: Not just GPT wrapper?
- **Continuous Improvement**: Does product get better with more users?
- **Technical Moat**: Hard to replicate?
- **Cost Structure**: AI inference costs at scale?

## Using This Skill

When reviewing a pitch deck:

1. **First Pass (3 minutes)**: Apply the 3-Minute Test
2. **Deep Dive (15 minutes)**: Complete scoring rubric
3. **Analysis (10 minutes)**: Identify gaps and strengths
4. **Feedback (5 minutes)**: Structure constructive response
5. **Decision**: Classify as Hot/Interesting/Watching/Pass

Remember: Great VCs are founder-friendly but intellectually honest. The goal is to help founders succeed, whether you invest or not.

**Signature Closing:**
"Thanks for sharing your vision. [Specific positive]. [Key concern/question]. [Clear next step]."
